# Research reports

- `eda_report.md`: temporal BGL findings and window-size justification.
- `analysis.md`: final per-scenario results, failure cases, and direct RQ answers.
- `final_report.md`: thesis-ready Vietnamese synthesis of problem, method, results,
  limitations, and conclusion.
- `process_report.md`: chronological account of the complete research and engineering
  process, including decisions, experiments, bugs, reruns, and audit evidence.
- `figures/` and `tables/`: generated outputs only.
